Key

Sunlight: short-wave energy. It passes through air.
Infrared heat: long-wave energy from the warm ground.
Greenhouse gases (CO₂, CH₄, H₂O): absorb infrared, then emit it in every direction, so some returns to the ground.
Absorb = take in energy. Emit = send out energy.
