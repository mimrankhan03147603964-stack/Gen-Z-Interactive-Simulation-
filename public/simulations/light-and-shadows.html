What is a Shadow?

A shadow appears when an object blocks light. The shadow is always formed on the side of the object that is away from the light source.

LightLight travels from a source in straight lines and illuminates objects.
ObjectAn opaque object does not let light pass through it.
ShadowA dark region appears behind the object, on the ground or on a wall.
