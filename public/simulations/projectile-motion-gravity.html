Projectile Motion & Classical Trajectory

A projectile moves under two independent 1D motions: Horizontal motion with constant velocity (zero acceleration) and Vertical motion governed by gravitational acceleration g.

Max Range (R):
R = (v₀² · sin 2θ) / g
Peak Height (H):
H = (v₀² · sin² θ) / (2g)
Time of Flight (T):
T = (2v₀ · sin θ) / g
