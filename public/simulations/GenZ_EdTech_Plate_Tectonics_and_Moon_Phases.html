🌍 Plate Tectonics: Definition

Plate tectonics is the idea that the hard outer shell of Earth is broken into huge pieces called plates. The plates float on hot, soft rock under them and move very slowly. Where the plates meet, we get mountains, volcanoes and earthquakes.

🌍 Plate Tectonics: Explanation

🔵 Divergent edge

Two plates move apart.

  1. Hot magma rises from the mantle into the gap.
  2. The magma cools and becomes new rock.
  3. New sea floor and a mountain ridge are made.

Example: Mid-Atlantic Ridge, East African Rift.

🔴 Convergent edge

Two plates move together.

  1. The heavy ocean plate sinks under the light land plate. This is called subduction.
  2. The sinking plate melts and makes magma.
  3. Magma rises to make volcanoes. Pushing makes mountains.

Example: Andes Mountains, Japan.

🟡 Transform edge

Two plates slide past each other.

  1. The plates get stuck on the rough edge.
  2. Stress builds up more and more.
  3. They slip all at once. The ground shakes. This is an earthquake!

Example: San Andreas Fault, USA.

Inside the Earth

  • Crust: the thin, hard skin. We live on it.
  • Mantle: thick, hot, soft rock. It slowly flows.
  • Outer core: very hot liquid metal.
  • Inner core: a solid metal ball, the hottest part.

🌙 Phases of the Moon: Definition

Moon phases are the different shapes of the bright part of the Moon that we see from Earth. The Moon makes no light of its own. It only reflects sunlight. Half of the Moon is always lit by the Sun, but as the Moon goes around Earth we see a different amount of the lit half.
29.5Days from one New Moon to the next
8Main phases
½The Moon is always half lit by the Sun
1Same side of the Moon always faces Earth

🌙 Phases of the Moon: Explanation

🌱 Waxing (growing bigger)

  1. New Moon: the Moon is between Earth and Sun. The lit side faces away, so we see no Moon.
  2. Each night a bit more of the right side shines. This is a crescent.
  3. First Quarter: half of the Moon we see is bright.
  4. More than half is bright. This is gibbous. Then comes the Full Moon.

🍂 Waning (getting smaller)

  1. Full Moon: Earth is between Sun and Moon. The whole face is bright.
  2. The bright part starts to shrink from the right side. Still more than half: gibbous.
  3. Last Quarter: again half is bright, on the left side.
  4. A thin crescent is left. Then it is a New Moon again.

These pictures are for people in the Northern Hemisphere. In the Southern Hemisphere the bright side is on the other side.
