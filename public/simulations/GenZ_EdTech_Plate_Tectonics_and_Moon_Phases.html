💡 Key Astronomical Facts

• Synodic Month: 29.53 days from New Moon to the next New Moon.

• Tidal Locking: The Moon takes 27.3 days to rotate and revolve, meaning the same face always points toward Earth.

• Spring Tides: Occur at New & Full Moons when gravitational pull of Sun and Moon align.
