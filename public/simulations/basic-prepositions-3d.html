English Prepositions: Comprehensive Reference Manual

Prepositions are words that establish relationships between substantives. Mastery of prepositions is key to natural, fluent, and mathematically precise communication in the English language.

📍 1. Prepositions of Place (Spatial Position)

Used to show where someone or something is situated in 3D space.

PrepositionSpatial MeaningCore Example
INEnclosed within a 3D boundary or volumeThe pencil is in the box.
ONResting upon an exterior surfaceThe clock is on the wall.
UNDERDirectly below or beneath a surfaceThe dog sleeps under the table.
BEHINDAt the rear side of a reference objectThe sun hid behind the cloud.
IN FRONT OFDirectly opposite the front sideThe teacher stands in front of the board.
BESIDEAt the side of; immediately next toShe sat beside her best friend.
BETWEENIn the middle of exactly two pointsThe ball stopped between two goalposts.
OVER / ABOVEHigher than, with vertical clearanceThe drone hovered over the crowd.

🚀 2. Prepositions of Movement & Direction

Used to express motion, velocity, and trajectory from one place to another.

PrepositionKinematic TrajectoryCore Example
INTOMovement entering an enclosed spaceShe stepped into the laboratory.
THROUGHMovement from one end to another inside a passageLight passed through the prism.
ACROSSMovement spanning from one side to the otherThe athlete swam across the river.
TOWARDSMotion facing in the direction of an objectThe car sped towards the exit.
OUT OFExiting from the interior of a containerThe bird flew out of the cage.
ONTOMovement landing on a surfaceThe cat leaped onto the counter.

⏳ 3. Prepositions of Time (Temporal Relations)

Used to position events along the arrow of time (At, On, In hierarchy).

PrepositionTemporal PrecisionCore Example
ATPrecise clock time, specific momentClass begins at 09:00 AM.
ONDays of the week, calendar datesThe exam is on Monday, Oct 12.
INMonths, years, seasons, long erasHumans landed on Moon in 1969.
DURINGThroughout the duration of an eventHe took notes during the lecture.
UNTILUp to a boundary point in timeThe library is open until 8 PM.
