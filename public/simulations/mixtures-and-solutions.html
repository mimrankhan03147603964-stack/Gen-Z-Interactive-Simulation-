💎 Mixtures & Solutions solute · solvent · concentration

Dissolve sugar or salt in water, stir, heat, and watch real particles move in real time. Observe saturation point and concentration change with every action.

📖 Definition

A mixture is a substance made of two or more materials combined without chemically joining. A solution is a special type of mixture in which one substance (the solute) dissolves evenly inside another (the solvent).

Teacher's Note: Stir sugar into water — the sugar dissolves and spreads evenly. That is a solution. Drop sand into water — it settles at the bottom. That is a suspension.

💡 Explanation

When solute particles spread evenly between solvent particles, a solution forms.

  • Solute: The substance that dissolves (sugar, salt).
  • Solvent: The liquid it dissolves into (water).
  • Concentration: How much solute is dissolved per volume.
  • Saturated: Maximum solute dissolved; no more can dissolve at current temperature.
  • Unsaturated: More solute can still dissolve.

🧩 Types of Mixtures

Solution

Even, transparent mix (sugar or salt water).

Suspension

Particles settle over time (sand or chalk in water).

Colloid

Stable intermediate dispersion (milk, gelatin).
