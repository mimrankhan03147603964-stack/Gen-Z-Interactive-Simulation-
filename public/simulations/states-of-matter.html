States of Matter

Everything in the universe is composed of matter. Observe how particles change kinetic behavior, spacing, and cohesion across solid, liquid, and gas phases.

What is Matter?

Matter is anything that possesses mass and occupies physical space. In everyday thermodynamic conditions, it manifests as solid, liquid, or gas.

Solid

Fixed shape • Fixed volume

Particles are tightly bound in an ordered lattice. Kinetic thermal motion is restricted to high-frequency in-place vibrations.

Liquid

Variable shape • Fixed volume

Particles remain in close proximity while freely sliding past each other, allowing fluid flow and surface adaptation.

Gas

Variable shape • Variable volume

Particles move at high velocities across wide intermolecular distances, rapidly diffusing to occupy the entire volume.
