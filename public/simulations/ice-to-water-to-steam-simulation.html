Solid State: Ice

Water molecules are arranged in a rigid hexagonal crystalline lattice bonded by intermolecular hydrogen bonds. The particles vibrate around fixed lattice points with low kinetic energy. Ice is less dense than liquid water due to open lattice geometry.

Key Thermodynamic Principles

• Latent Heat of Fusion (334 kJ/kg): Heat absorbed to melt ice into water without changing temperature.
• Latent Heat of Vaporization (2260 kJ/kg): Heat required to turn boiling water into steam.
• Kinetic Molecular Theory: Average kinetic energy of particles is directly proportional to absolute temperature (Kelvin).
