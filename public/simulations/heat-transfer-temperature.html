1. Heat Transfer

Conduction, Convection & Radiation — See How Heat Moves

Click one button at a time. The simulation shows heat movement with clear, colourful examples.

Simple Definition

Heat transfer is the movement of thermal energy from a hotter object or area to a colder one.

Simple Explanation

Heat naturally moves from hot → cold. It can move through direct contact, moving fluids, or electromagnetic waves.

Three Methods of Heat Transfer

🔩 Conduction

Heat moves through direct contact. Example: a metal spoon becomes hot in hot tea.

🍲 Convection

Heat moves by the movement of a liquid or gas. Example: warm air rises.

☀️ Radiation

Heat travels as waves. Example: heat from the Sun reaches Earth.

2. Temperature

Hot, Cold, Celsius Scale & Particle Movement

Move the temperature slider. The thermometer, the water state and the particle movement change together.

Simple Definition

Temperature tells us how hot or cold something is. It is commonly measured in degrees Celsius (°C).

Simple Explanation

Temperature is related to the average motion of particles. When particles move faster, the temperature is generally higher. When they move more slowly, the temperature is lower.

Celsius Temperature Scale

❄️ Below 0°C

Water is normally solid (ice).

🧊 0°C

Water reaches its freezing point.

🏠 Room Temperature

About 20–25°C is comfortable indoor temperature.

💨 100°C

Water reaches its boiling point at standard pressure.
