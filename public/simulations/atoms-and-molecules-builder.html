Subatomic Architecture

Protons define atomic number (Z). Neutrons supply binding nuclear force. Electrons (e⁻) fill shells (2, 8, 8, 18) according to the Pauli exclusion principle.

Shell Capacities

  • K-Shell (n=1): Maximum 2 electrons
  • L-Shell (n=2): Maximum 8 electrons
  • M-Shell (n=3): Maximum 8 electrons (for elements up to Argon)

Stability & Charge

An atom is electrically neutral when protons equal electrons. If electrons are gained or lost, an ion is formed (+ cation or − anion).

Atoms

The fundamental building blocks of all chemical elements. Smallest particle retaining elemental properties.

Molecules

Two or more atoms bonded covalently. Can be homonuclear (O₂) or heteronuclear compounds (H₂O).

Compounds vs Mixtures

Compounds have fixed chemical ratios with chemical bonds. Mixtures are physical combinations without fixed proportions.
