Geometry, Polygon Architecture & Angles Euclidean Geometry • Angles • Geometric Transformations

A polygon is a closed two-dimensional plane figure bounded by a finite number of straight line segments. The sum of interior angles in an $n$-sided polygon follows the universal formula $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$.

Fundamental Laws of Polygons

  • Interior Angle Sum: $S = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$. Triangle ($n=3$): $180^\circ$; Quadrilateral ($n=4$): $360^\circ$; Pentagon ($n=5$): $540^\circ$.
  • Regular Polygon: All sides are congruent and all interior angles are equal: $\text{Each Angle} = \frac{(n-2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$.
  • Exterior Angles: For any convex polygon, the sum of all exterior angles is always constant at $360^\circ$.

Angle Classification

Acute: $< 90^\circ$ • Right: $= 90^\circ$ • Obtuse: $90^\circ < \theta < 180^\circ$ • Straight: $= 180^\circ$ Complementary angles sum to $90^\circ$; Supplementary angles sum to $180^\circ$.

Pythagorean Theorem

In any right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse $c$ equals the sum of the squares of the other two legs $a$ and $b$ ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$).

Real-World Utility

  • Architecture & Civil Engineering: Triangulation structures (trusses in bridges and geodesic domes) offer unmatched structural rigidity.
  • GPS Navigation & Cartography: Triangular trilateration determines precise smartphone coordinates via satellite signal vectors.

Protractor Measuring & Degree Standard

A full circle rotation is partitioned into 360 equal angular degrees ($360^\circ$).

Acute Angles

Between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. Sharp, piercing angular profiles.

Right Angles

Exactly $90^\circ$ ($\pi/2$ radians). Denotes perpendicular orthogonality.

Obtuse & Reflex

Obtuse: $90^\circ - 180^\circ$. Reflex: greater than $180^\circ$ but less than $360^\circ$.

Pythagorean Triples & Proofs

Whole integer solutions ($a, b, c$) satisfying $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

Triple (3, 4, 5)

$3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$. Used by ancient Egyptian rope-stretchers.

Triple (5, 12, 13)

$5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13^2$.

Triple (8, 15, 17)

$8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17^2$.
