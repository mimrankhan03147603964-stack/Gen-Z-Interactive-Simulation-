Evaporation & Transpiration Phase 1

Solar radiation warms liquid water in oceans, lakes, and rivers, supplying latent heat that transforms water molecules into invisible water vapor. Simultaneously, plants and vegetation transpire moisture through microscopic leaf stomata into the atmosphere.

Hydrologic Cycle Stages
1. Evaporation (Ocean / Lakes) Solar Heat
2. Transpiration (Forest Canopy) Biosphere
3. Condensation (Clouds) Cooling
4. Precipitation (Rain & Snow) Gravity
5. Surface Runoff & Rivers Water Flow
6. Infiltration & Aquifer Underground
