Simple Machines — 6 Mechanical Tools

Learn how humans multiply force with Levers, Pulleys, Ramps, Wedges, Screws & Wheels

Definitions — What is a Simple Machine?

A Simple Machine is a basic mechanical device that makes work easier by changing the direction or magnitude of a force.

1. Lever — A bar turning on a fulcrum

A rigid bar that rotates about a fixed fulcrum. Formula: MA = Effort Arm ÷ Load Arm.

MA = Effort Arm ÷ Load Arm

2. Pulley — Wheel with a grooved rope

A grooved wheel carrying a rope. Formula: MA = Number of supporting rope sections.

MA = Number of Supporting Ropes

3. Inclined Plane — Slanted surface

A ramp that trades distance for force. Formula: MA = Length ÷ Height.

MA = Ramp Length ÷ Height

4. Wedge — Dual inclined plane

Two joined ramps that convert downward drive into lateral splitting force.

MA = Length ÷ Width

5. Screw — Spiral ramp around cylinder

Converts rotational torque into linear thrust with immense mechanical advantage.

MA = 2πr ÷ Pitch

6. Wheel & Axle — Continuous rotary lever

Larger wheel turns smaller shaft, multiplying applied hand torque.

MA = Wheel Radius ÷ Axle Radius
