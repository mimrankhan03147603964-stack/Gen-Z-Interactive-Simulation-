Key

Pascal's Principle: pressure applied to a trapped liquid is passed on equally in every direction.
Pressure P = F ÷ A (force divided by area).
Big force from small force: F₂ = F₁ × (A₂ ÷ A₁).
Trade-off: the large piston moves less than the small one, so no energy is gained.
