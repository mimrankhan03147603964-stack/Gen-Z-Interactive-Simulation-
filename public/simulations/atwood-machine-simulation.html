Atwood machine: two masses hang from a string over a pulley. The heavier mass moves down and the lighter one moves up, with the same acceleration a = (m2 − m1)g / (m1 + m2). The string tension is T = 2m1m2g / (m1 + m2). Press Show Forces to see weight (W) and tension (T). The pulley and string are treated as having no mass and no friction.
