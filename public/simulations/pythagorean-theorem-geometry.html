The Pythagorean Theorem Formula

In any right-angled Euclidean triangle, the area of the square whose side is the hypotenuse (c) is equal to the sum of the areas of the squares on the other two sides (a and b).

a² + b² = c²   ⟹   c = √(a² + b²)
📐 Right Triangle: a = 3 • b = 4 • c = 5 (3² + 4² = 5²)
