📖 Definition

Photosynthesis is the biological process by which green plants utilize solar energy, water, and carbon dioxide to synthesize glucose and release oxygen.

💡 Explanation

Chlorophyll in leaf cells captures solar photon energy. Roots draw water and dissolved minerals through the xylem. Atmospheric CO₂ enters via leaf stomata. The plant produces glucose for cellular energy and releases O₂.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Solar Energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ (Glucose) + 6O₂

🌱 Key Facts

Where?

Inside chloroplasts within leaf mesophyll cells.

Chlorophyll

Light-capturing pigment molecules.

Stomata

Pores regulating gas diffusion (CO₂ in, O₂ out).

Xylem

Vascular tissue conducting water from roots upward.
