The 8 Lunar Phases & Tidal Bulges

As the Moon orbits Earth every 29.5 days (Synodic Month), sunlight illuminates half its surface. The phase we observe depends on the Moon's orbital angle relative to the Earth-Sun vector.

1. New Moon (Day 0)
2. Waxing Crescent (Day 3.7)
3. First Quarter (Day 7.4)
4. Waxing Gibbous (Day 11.1)
5. Full Moon (Day 14.8)
6. Waning Gibbous (Day 18.5)
7. Third Quarter (Day 22.1)
8. Waning Crescent (Day 25.8)
