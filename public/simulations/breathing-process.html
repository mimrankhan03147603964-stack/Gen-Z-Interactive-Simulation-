Definition

Breathing is the process of taking air into the lungs (inhalation) and pushing it out again (exhalation). It brings fresh oxygen (O₂) into the body and takes waste carbon dioxide (CO₂) out of the body.
O₂Oxygen goes IN when we inhale
CO₂Carbon dioxide goes OUT when we exhale
12 - 20Breaths every minute

Explanation

Inhalation (breathing in)

  1. The diaphragm, a muscle under the lungs, contracts and moves down.
  2. The chest gets bigger, so the lungs expand.
  3. Air pressure inside drops, so fresh air (oxygen) enters through the nose or mouth.
  4. Air goes down the trachea to the alveoli, where oxygen passes into the blood.

Exhalation (breathing out)

  1. The diaphragm relaxes and moves up.
  2. The chest gets smaller, so the lungs compress.
  3. Air pressure inside rises, so used air exits.
  4. Carbon dioxide from the blood leaves the alveoli and goes out through the nose or mouth.
