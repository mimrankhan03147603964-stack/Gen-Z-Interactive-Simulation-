⚛️ Atoms & Molecules building blocks of matter

Watch atoms vibrate, slide, and fly. Bond them into molecules. Heat or cool them to change state.

📖 Definition

Atoms are the smallest particles of a chemical element. Molecules are groups of two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds.

Teacher's Note: Cut chalk in half again and again — you reach an atom. Two hydrogen + one oxygen = water molecule (H₂O).

💡 Explanation

Everything is made of atoms. The state of matter depends on how tightly atoms are packed and how much they move.

  • Solid: atoms vibrate in fixed positions.
  • Liquid: atoms slide past each other.
  • Gas: atoms move freely and fill space.

🧩 Types of Matter

Elements

One type of atom. Examples: Oxygen (O), Gold (Au).

Compounds

Different atoms bonded. Examples: Water (H₂O), Salt (NaCl).

Mixtures

Mixed but not joined. Examples: Air, salad.

💡 Light reflection, refraction & spectrum

A real laser beam, a mirror, a glass block, and a prism — all drawn clearly with labels.

📖 Definition

Light is electromagnetic radiation visible to the human eye. It travels in straight lines.

💡 Explanation

Light can be reflected (bounced), refracted (bent), and dispersed (split into colours).

  • Reflection: Angle in = Angle out. Mirrors use this.
  • Refraction: Light bends entering glass or water.
  • Spectrum: White light splits into 7 colours.

🌈 Key Properties

Reflection

Mirror bounces light back at same angle.

Refraction

Light bends when entering glass.

Spectrum

Prism splits white light into ROYGBIV.

🔊 Sound vibrations, pitch & loudness

A speaker cone vibrates and pushes air particles. Change frequency and amplitude.

📖 Definition

Sound is a vibration that travels as a mechanical wave through a medium. It cannot travel in a vacuum.

💡 Explanation

Vibrating objects create pressure waves. Frequency = pitch. Amplitude = loudness.

  • Pitch: High frequency = high pitch.
  • Loudness: Large amplitude = loud sound.
  • Speed: Fastest in solids, slowest in gases.

🎚️ Key Features

Pitch

Frequency determines pitch.

Loudness

Amplitude determines loudness.

Speed

Fastest in solids, slowest in gases.

🔄 Pressure force per unit area

A piston presses down on a surface. Change force and area to see pressure change.

📖 Definition

Pressure is force applied perpendicular to a surface per unit area. Measured in pascals (Pa).

💡 Explanation

Pressure = Force ÷ Area. Smaller area = more pressure.

  • Force & Area: More force or less area = more pressure.
  • Atmospheric: Weight of air, ~101 kPa at sea level.
  • Liquid: Pressure increases with depth.

📊 Key Concepts

Force & Area

More force or less area = more pressure.

Atmospheric

Weight of air, ~101 kPa.

Liquid

Pressure increases with depth.
