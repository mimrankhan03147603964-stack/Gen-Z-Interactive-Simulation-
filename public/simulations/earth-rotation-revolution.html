Rotation

Rotation is the spinning of the Earth on its own axis. Earth completes one full spin in about 24 hours. This gives us day and night.

☀️

Revolution

Revolution is the journey of the Earth around the Sun. Earth completes one trip in about 365 and a quarter days. This is one year, and it gives us seasons.

📍

Axis

The axis is an imaginary line that goes through the North Pole and the South Pole. The Earth spins around this line. The axis is tilted by 23.5 degrees.

⭕

Orbit

The orbit is the path that the Earth follows around the Sun. It looks like a big oval circle. The Earth always moves along this path.

🌗

Why do we have day and night?

The Sun lights only one half of the Earth at a time. The bright half has day. The dark half has night. As the Earth rotates, every place moves from the bright side to the dark side.

➡️

Which way does the Earth spin?

The Earth spins from west to east. That is why the Sun looks like it rises in the east and sets in the west.

🍂

Why do we have seasons?

The Earth's axis is tilted 23.5 degrees and always points to the North Star. When the North Pole leans toward the Sun, the north has summer. When it leans away, the north has winter.

📅

Year and leap year

One trip around the Sun takes 365 days and about 6 hours. We add one extra day to February every 4 years. That year is called a leap year and has 366 days.

⚡

How fast is the Earth?

At the equator, the Earth spins at about 1,670 kilometres per hour. Around the Sun, it travels at about 107,000 kilometres per hour. We do not feel it because everything moves together.
