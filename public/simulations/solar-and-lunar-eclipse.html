📖 Ray Optics & Key Concepts

• Umbra: The inner darkest cone of the shadow where sunlight is completely blocked.

• Penumbra: The outer partial shadow where only a portion of the solar disk is obscured.

• Blood Moon: During a lunar eclipse, Earth's atmosphere bends red wavelengths into the shadow via Rayleigh scattering, casting a coppery red glow onto the Moon.
