Why does water have surface tension?
A molecule deep inside water is pulled equally in all directions by hydrogen bonds. A molecule at the surface has no neighbours above, so it is pulled sideways and downward. The surface behaves like a stretched skin. Soap breaks many hydrogen bonds, so the skin gets weaker.
