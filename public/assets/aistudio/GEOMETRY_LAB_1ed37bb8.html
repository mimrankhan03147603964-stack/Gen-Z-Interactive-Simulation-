🔷 2D Shapes area & perimeter explorer

Choose a shape, drag the sliders to resize it, and instantly see area, perimeter, and formulas update.

📖 Definition

2D shapes are flat figures with only two dimensions — length and width. They have area (space inside) and perimeter (distance around the edge).

Teacher's Note: Every shape has a unique formula. Rectangle = length × width, Triangle = ½ × base × height. Practice identifying each shape by its sides and angles.

💡 Formulas

  • Rectangle: Area = l × w, Perimeter = 2(l + w)
  • Square: Area = s², Perimeter = 4s
  • Triangle: Area = ½ × b × h
  • Circle: Area = πr², Circumference = 2πr
  • Parallelogram: Area = b × h
  • Trapezium: Area = ½(a + b) × h

🧩 Types of 2D Shapes

Rectangle

4 sides, opposite sides equal, 4 right angles.

Square

4 equal sides, 4 right angles.

Triangle

3 sides, angles sum to 180°.

Circle

All points equidistant from centre.

Parallelogram

Opposite sides parallel and equal.

Trapezium

One pair of parallel sides.

📐 Angles types & measurement

See acute, right, obtuse, straight, and reflex angles drawn clearly with an animated rotating arm.

📖 Definition

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common point called the vertex. It is measured in degrees (°).

Teacher's Note: Hold your arms out — the gap between them is an angle. A full turn = 360°, half turn = 180°, quarter turn = 90°.

💡 Types of Angles

  • Acute: Less than 90° (sharp corner)
  • Right: Exactly 90° (corner of a book)
  • Obtuse: Between 90° and 180°
  • Straight: Exactly 180° (a line)
  • Reflex: Between 180° and 360°
  • Full: Exactly 360° (full circle)

🌍 Real-Life Examples

Acute

Pizza slice tip, clock at 2 o'clock.

Right

Door frame, laptop corner.

Obtuse

Open laptop wider, sofa back.

Straight

Road, ruler edge.

Reflex

Steering wheel turn, door opening wide.

Full

Fan rotation, clock hands full circle.

🔺 Triangles types & angle sum

Explore equilateral, isosceles, scalene, and right triangles. The three angles always add up to 180°.

📖 Definition

A triangle is a 3-sided polygon. The sum of its interior angles is always 180°.

Teacher's Note: Tear the three corners of a paper triangle and place them together — they always form a straight line (180°).

💡 Properties

  • Sum of interior angles = 180°
  • Sum of exterior angles = 360°
  • Longest side is opposite the largest angle
  • Equilateral = all sides and angles equal (60° each)
  • Isosceles = two sides equal, two angles equal
  • Scalene = all sides and angles different

🔺 Types of Triangles

Equilateral

All 3 sides equal, all angles 60°.

Isosceles

2 sides equal, 2 base angles equal.

Scalene

No equal sides, no equal angles.

Right-Angled

One angle is exactly 90°.

Acute

All angles less than 90°.

Obtuse

One angle greater than 90°.

⭕ Circles parts & properties

Explore radius, diameter, chord, tangent, secant, arc, and sector — all clearly labelled with colours.

📖 Definition

A circle is a set of all points at an equal distance (radius) from a fixed point (centre).

Teacher's Note: π (pi) ≈ 3.14159. It is the ratio of circumference to diameter for ANY circle.

💡 Parts of a Circle

  • Radius: Centre to edge
  • Diameter: Across through centre (= 2r)
  • Chord: Line joining two points on circle
  • Tangent: Touches circle at one point
  • Secant: Cuts circle at two points
  • Arc: Part of the circumference
  • Sector: "Pizza slice" region

📊 Formulas

Circumference

C = 2πr = πd

Area

A = πr²

Arc Length

(θ/360°) × 2πr

Sector Area

(θ/360°) × πr²

Chord

c = 2√(r² − d²)

Tangent

Perpendicular to radius at contact

📏 Pythagoras Theorem a² + b² = c²

Drag the sliders to change the two legs. See the hypotenuse and the areas of the three squares update live.

📖 Definition

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares of the other two sides: a² + b² = c².

Teacher's Note: Used by builders for thousands of years to make perfect right angles — the "3-4-5" rule.

💡 Explanation

  • a and b are the two shorter sides (legs)
  • c is the longest side (hypotenuse), opposite the right angle
  • Only works for right-angled triangles
  • Common triples: (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17), (7,24,25)

🔍 Visual Proof

The area of the square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the areas of the squares on the other two sides.

Small Square

Area = a² (on side a)

Medium Square

Area = b² (on side b)

Large Square

Area = c² = a² + b²

🔄 Transformations reflection, rotation, translation, enlargement

See how a shape moves on a coordinate grid. Choose the transformation and watch the shape and its image.

📖 Definition

A transformation changes the position, size, or orientation of a shape on a coordinate plane.

Teacher's Note: The original shape is called the object; the new shape is the image.

💡 Types of Transformations

  • Reflection: Mirror image over a line
  • Rotation: Turn around a fixed point
  • Translation: Slide without turning
  • Enlargement: Resize by a scale factor

📊 Rules

Reflection

Over x-axis: (x, y) → (x, −y)

Rotation 90°

(x, y) → (−y, x)

Rotation 180°

(x, y) → (−x, −y)

Translation

(x, y) → (x + a, y + b)

Enlargement

(x, y) → (kx, ky)

Reflection y=x

(x, y) → (y, x)
