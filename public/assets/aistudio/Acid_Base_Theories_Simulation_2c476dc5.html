Arrhenius theory
Acid: a substance that gives H⁺ ions in water.
Base: a substance that gives OH⁻ ions in water.
H⁺ and OH⁻ join to form water: this is neutralisation.

Brønsted-Lowry theory
Acid: proton (H⁺) donor.
Base: proton acceptor.
After the transfer, the acid becomes its conjugate base and the base becomes its conjugate acid. A conjugate pair differs by one H⁺.

Lewis theory
Acid: an electron-pair acceptor (has an empty orbital).
Base: an electron-pair donor (has a lone pair).
They share the pair in a coordinate (dative) bond. This theory also covers acids without H, such as BF₃.
