Rutherford's conclusions
1. Most particles pass straight: an atom is mostly empty space.
2. Some bend: the centre is positively charged.
3. A few bounce back: the centre (nucleus) is tiny, dense and heavy.
4. Electrons move around the nucleus.
Note: in real life only about 1 in 8000 particles rebounds. This simulation exaggerates it so you can see it.

Bohr's postulates
1. Electrons move in fixed circular orbits (shells) without losing energy.
2. Each shell has a fixed energy: K (n=1), L (n=2), M (n=3), N (n=4).
3. An electron absorbs energy to jump up and emits energy as light when it falls down. Photon energy = Ehigh − Elow.
