💧 Water Cycle continuous circulation of water

Ocean → Evaporation → Condensation → Precipitation → Runoff → Collection.

📖 Definition

The Water Cycle describes the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth, driven by solar energy and gravity.

💡 Explanation

Solar heating causes ocean water evaporation. Vapor ascends, cools, and undergoes condensation into clouds. Droplets precipitate as rain, returning via river runoff and underground infiltration.

🌊 The Seven Stages

1. Evaporation

Liquid water becomes vapor.

2. Condensation

Vapor forms cloud droplets.

3. Precipitation

Water falls as rain or snow.

4. Collection

Water returns to oceans and lakes.

🌍 Day & Night Earth's rotation

The Earth rotates on its axis once every 24 hours relative to the Sun.

📖 Definition

Day and Night occur as a spherical planet rotates on its polar axis in sunlight. The sunlit hemisphere experiences daytime; the shadow hemisphere experiences nighttime.

💡 Explanation

Earth rotates eastward on its 23.5°-tilted axis every 24 hours. The boundary separating day and night is called the terminator line.
