Multiplication Tables
CLASS 3 • MATHS

Multiplication Tables

Multiplication is a fast way of adding the same number again and again. A table shows the answers when we multiply one number by 1, 2, 3 … up to 10.

3 × 4 = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12
👥

Equal Groups

Same number in every group

3 baskets with 4 mangoes in each basket is 3 × 4. Every group must have the same amount.

➕

Repeated Addition

Add again and again

4 + 4 + 4 = 12 is the same as 3 × 4 = 12. Multiplication is just quicker!

📋

Times Table

A list of answers

The table of 5 is 5, 10, 15, 20 … Each answer grows by 5.

🦘

Skip Counting

Jump by the same number

Table of 3 means jumping in 3s.

3 → 6 → 9 → 12 → 15Table of 10: 10, 20, 30, 40 …Table of 2: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 …
🔄

Swap the Numbers

Order does not change the answer

3 × 4 and 4 × 3 both make 12. Learn one and you know two!

2 × 7 = 7 × 2 = 145 × 6 = 6 × 5 = 30Rows and columns give the same total
⚡

Magic Tricks

Learn tables faster

Use these easy patterns to remember answers.

×2 → double the number×5 → ends in 0 or 5×10 → put a zero at the end
REAL LIFE

5 packets with 6 biscuits each? That is 5 × 6 = 30 biscuits. We use multiplication in shops, at school and at home!
